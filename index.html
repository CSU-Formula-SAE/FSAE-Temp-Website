About Formula Vikes

Formula Vikes Racing is Cleveland State University’s Formula SAE collegiate design team based in the Washkewicz College of Engineering. We are an interdisciplinary collective of dedicated student engineers designing, fabricating, testing, and racing high-performance open-wheel Formula-style race cars on the world stage.
